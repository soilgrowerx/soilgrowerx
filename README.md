# George Steward
**ISA Certified Arborist (TX-362025A) | Founder, Soil Grower | MNR Candidate, Oregon State University**

The woods work on deep time. Most of what I do now is built around that fact.

My work centers on forest ecology, wildland systems, and practical land stewardship under changing climate conditions. I view working landscapes and wildlands as living communities rather than ornamental spaces, prioritizing canopy preservation, root zone architecture, and living soil biology over cosmetic tree work.

### Operational Discipline to Ecological Systems
Before entering natural resources, I served in the United States Army with 7th Special Forces Group as an Environmental Liaison and Information Systems Supervisor, leading enterprise information technology operations, logistical coordination, and technical teams under field conditions. Following active duty, I served as a Clay Hunt Fellow with Team Rubicon, applying Incident Command System protocols and operational discipline to disaster response and community resilience operations.

Today I bring that same systems mindset to the land:
* **Soil Grower (Central Texas):** Founded in 2024 as a Disabled Veteran Owned Business focused on urban reforestation, diagnostic tree appraisal, supersonic pneumatic soil excavation (AirSpade 2000), root collar remediation, oak wilt management, and rhizosphere biology restoration.
* **Applied Heat and Drought Research:** Over the past five years across Arizona and Texas, I have studied how sustained heat and moisture deficit affect living systems. What held up in the field informed Soil Grower's biochar and soil conditioning practice, focused on increasing soil water retention and native plant survival in degraded, drought-prone soils.
* **Graduate Scholarship (Oregon State University):** Completing a Master of Natural Resources (MNR) degree in Forests and Climate Change (building on the graduate Forests and Climate Change certificate and a B.S. in Environmental Science).

### Active Public Repositories & Open Standards
* [**`soilgrowerx/subterranean-field-records`**](https://github.com/soilgrowerx/subterranean-field-records): Open spatial dataset (`GeoJSON`), `ASAE S313.3` soil compaction primers, and root zone restoration schemas across Central Texas watersheds (`55,050 sq ft` evaluated `CRZ`, `48,050 sq ft` pneumatic decompaction across `17` documented case corridors).
* [**`soilgrowerx/wildland-policy-and-ecology`**](https://github.com/soilgrowerx/wildland-policy-and-ecology): Graduate natural resource policy scholarship (*The Prudent Man and the Public Trust: Redefining 'Valuable Mineral Deposits' under the General Mining Act of 1872*), full-cost ecosystem service valuation schemas, and field drought resilience protocols.
* [**`Arboracle-Ecosystem/openarbor`**](https://github.com/Arboracle-Ecosystem/openarbor): Open-standards arboricultural engine (`v2.0`) providing zero-dependency `CRZ` / `TPZ` (`ANSI A300` / `ISA BMP` / `AS 4970`) calculators, `Nowak` / `McPherson` allometric ecosystem benefit models, `John White (FCIN12)` veteran tree age estimation, and data-driven municipal tree ordinance compliance (`GeoJSON` ready).
